Your super meter fills over time, and faster when you concede. When it is full, press your super key while you have the ball. Every team has its own super move. In 1 player mode the arrow keys also move Player 1. A tackle knocks the ball off a close opponent. A special formation lasts 12 seconds, then needs 25 seconds to recharge. Press Esc during a match to pause.

Team stats

Speed changes how fast a team runs, attack changes shot power and accuracy, and defense changes how often tackles win the ball.
